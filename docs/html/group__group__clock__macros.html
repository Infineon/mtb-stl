Macro Definition Documentation

◆ CLOCK_TICKS_LO

#define CLOCK_TICKS_LO   (20u)

Lower possible clock count for WDT depending on the accuracy of oscillator.

  • PSOC 4 devices: 20u, calculated for PSOC 4100S Max with ILO = 40 kHz: 40 * (1 - 50%) = 20
  • PSOC 61 Programmable Line, PSOC 62 Performance Line, PSOC Control C3, and XMC7000 devices: 31u, calculated with WCO = 32 kHz: 32 * (1 - 0.015%) = 31
  • XMC5000 devices: 30u, calculated with WCO = 32.77 kHz: 32.77 * (1 - 7%) = 30

◆ CLOCK_TICKS_HI

#define CLOCK_TICKS_HI   (80u)

Higher possible clock count for WDT depending on the accuracy of oscillator.

  • PSOC 4 devices: 80u, calculated for PSOC 4100S Max with ILO = 40 kHz: 40 * (1 + 100%) = 80
  • PSOC 61 Programmable Line, PSOC 62 Performance Line, PSOC Control C3, and XMC7000 devices: 33u, calculated with WCO = 32 kHz: 32 * (1 + 0.015%) = 33
  • XMC5000 devices: 36u, calculated with WCO = 32.77 kHz: 32.77 * (1 + 7%) = 36

◆ CLOCK_TEST_TIME_TIMER_PERIOD

#define CLOCK_TEST_TIME_TIMER_PERIOD    (((CY_CFG_SYSCLK_IMO_FREQ_HZ/(1000000uL)) * (CLOCK_TEST_TIME)) / CLOCK_TEST_TIMER_CLK_DIV)

Number of timer/source-clock cycles corresponding to CLOCK_TEST_TIME.

This macro is used on PSOC 4 devices. PSOC 61 Programmable Line, PSOC 62 Performance Line, PSOC Control C3, XMC7000, and XMC5000 devices calculate the equivalent period at runtime from the peripheral or high-frequency clock and its divider.
