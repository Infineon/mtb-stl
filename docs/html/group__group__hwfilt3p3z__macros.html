◆ HWFILT3P3Z_ATT_RATIO

#define HWFILT3P3Z_ATT_RATIO   (8U)

Required attenuation ratio for the out-of-band sub-test: low_peak must be strictly greater than HWFILT3P3Z_ATT_RATIO * high_peak.

The theoretical LPF attenuation ratio at the test frequencies is ~74:1, so a threshold of 8 provides a large safety margin. Shared by the PPCA and HPPASS variants.
